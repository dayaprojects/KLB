## 1. Principles

1. **One framework, two varieties, three editions.** The curriculum strands are shared. Grammar, pronunciation and orthography items are **split by variety** where the languages differ, and they are delivered in each edition's script (`ckb` Arabic script; `kmr-Latn`; `kmr-Arab` from Phase 2).
2. **Minimum core vs extension.** The *minimum core* is mandatory for every Level-N book, course and Hub module. Each core item maps to a unit and a descriptor (D13). *Extensions* are optional (regional vocabulary, culture boxes, extra texts) and are never required for the level check.
3. **Same descriptors as KBS** (D2 §3), behind a firewall: the Series teaches the construct, never the item bank (DN-22).
4. **Communicative and visual first** (EFE parity). Grammar is introduced through formation diagrams after a meaningful context.
5. **Spiky profiles welcome.** Heritage learners use the Heritage Fast-Track (D15) to prioritise literacy, without being held back on oral skills.

## 6. Subjects (adult thematic syllabus) and sensitivity rules

| Band | Subjects (§16.5) | Placed in |
|---|---|---|
| A1–A2 | Greetings and introductions; family; home and neighbourhood; daily routine; food, drink and Kurdish cuisine; shopping, bazaar and money; time and calendar (Kurdish month names, regional variants); transport and directions; weather and seasons; body and health; clothes; leisure; hospitality and etiquette | L1, L2 |
| B1 | Work and professions; education; travel in Kurdistan and the diaspora; public services and paperwork; media and technology; festivals and traditions (Newroz); housing; nature and environment | L3 |
| B2 | Society and community; current affairs (neutral framing); history and heritage; literature and oral tradition (dengbêj, folk tales); arts and music; science and health; workplace communication; opinion and debate | L4 |
| C1 | Academic discourse; classical and modern literature; law and administration; media analysis | L5 |

**Representation rules** (bias and sensitivity review, D2 §5.2, applied to the Series):
- **Regions:** every level includes settings from Iraq/KRI, Turkey, Iran, Syria and the diaspora. Each region appears in at least 10 % of units, and no region in more than 50 %.
- **Communities:** Muslim, Yazidi, Christian, Kaka'i/Yarsan, Alevi, Jewish-heritage and secular characters appear in everyday roles. There are no stereotypes and no religious instruction.
- **Gender:** speaking roles are balanced within 45–55 %. Women and men appear in professional and domestic roles alike.
- **No partisan content:** no party symbols, leaders or contested political claims. History is presented through cultural heritage and multiple perspectives (B2+).
- **Trauma-aware:** no graphic violence, displacement or conflict scenes before B2. From B2 on, these topics appear only with care notes for teachers.
