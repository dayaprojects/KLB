## 7. Other titles (chapter-level outlines)

| Title | Outline |
|---|---|
| **Grammar Guide + Practice** (per variety) | Part A Sounds and script · B Nouns (definiteness, plural, ezafe; kmr gender and case) · C Pronouns and clitics · D Adjectives and comparison · E Verbs: stems, present, imperative, subjunctive, past intransitive, **past transitive and ergativity**, perfect tenses, passive, causative, compound verbs · F Modality · G Prepositions and circumpositions · H Clauses: relative, conditional, temporal, reported speech · I Discourse and register · J Orthography reference. | **Vocabulary Builder** | About 100 illustrated thematic spreads (A1–B2): home, family, food, bazaar, transport, health, work, education, nature, festivals, media, society … Every headword has audio; regional variants are boxed. |
| **Heritage Fast-Track** | Module 1 (A2): reading what you can say: script, spelling of spoken forms, from dialect to written norm (AVP-based). Module 2 (B1): reading and writing connected text; register awareness (formal vs home language). Module 3 (B2): literacy for work and study; formal writing. Speaking tasks build on existing oral fluency; literacy is the main focus. |
| **Teacher's Guide** (per level) | Unit-by-unit lesson plans (60–90 min); classroom adaptations of Course Book exercises; differentiation for heritage, L1-schooled-in-other-language and L2 learners; formative assessment; Hub use; photocopiable extra activities; teacher notes on sensitive topics. |
| **Kurdish for Work** | Workplace communication: meetings, email, phone, presentations, customer service, reports, sector vocabulary (NGO and humanitarian, health, education, public administration, business). |
| **Sorani ↔ Kurmanji Bridge** | 24 units, each pairing the same situation in both varieties: sound correspondences, false friends, grammar contrasts (clitics vs oblique, ezafe forms, ergative marking), reading the other script, listening to the other variety. Prepares for the Phase 3 cross-variety comprehension endorsement (AD-001). |
| **Level 5 / Academic Kurdish** | Academic reading and writing; lecture listening; seminar discussion; literary and classical texts (with modern commentary); stylistics; research language. |
| **Junior series** | Defined in Phase 3 with the KBS Junior construct; guardian-oriented, play-based. |
