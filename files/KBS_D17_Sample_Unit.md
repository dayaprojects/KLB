## 2. Spread 2 — Formation diagrams + E01–E03

### 2.1 Formation diagram (Sorani; reads right to left; colours per D16 §3.5)

```
  من        +   خەڵکی    +   هەولێر    +   ـم           →   من خەڵکی هەولێرم.
 [pronoun]     [people of]   [STEM: blue]   [ENDING: green]
```

| Person | Ending (after a consonant) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| من (I) | ـم | من خەڵکی هەولێرم. |
| تۆ (you) | ـیت | تۆ خەڵکی هەولێریت؟ |
| ئەو (he/she) | ـە (after a vowel: ـیە) | ئەو خەڵکی کەرکووکە. · ئەو خەڵکی ئەڵمانیایە. |
| Negative | نیم · نیت · نییە | من خەڵکی هەولێر نیم. |

**Tip box:** «خەڵکی» means "people of". Sorani says "I am (of the) people of Erbil".

### 2.2 Formation diagram (Kurmanji; left to right)

```
  Ez   +   ji   +   Duhok  +  -ê          +   me              →   Ez ji Duhokê me.
 [pronoun] [from]  [STEM: blue] [OBLIQUE: teal]  [ENDING: green]
```

| Person | After a consonant | After a vowel | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| ez (I) | im | me | Ez Rêber im. · Ez ji Duhokê me. |
| tu (you) | î | yî | Tu ji ku derê yî? |
| ew (he/she) | e | ye | Ew Daniel e. · Ew ji Zaxoyê ye. |
| Negative | ne + … | | Ez ne ji Hewlêrê me. |

**Tip box:** after «ji», place names take **-ê**: Duhok → ji Duhok**ê**; Zaxo → ji Zaxo**yê**.
